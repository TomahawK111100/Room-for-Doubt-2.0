\section{Teacher comparison on CIFAR-10.2}
\label{sec:teacher-cifar102}
Following the CIFAR-10.1 evaluation, we fixed a separate protocol for
direct distillation on the same three frozen CIFAR-10N Worse bases.
The external test used all 2,000 images in the official CIFAR-10.2
test split~\citep{lu2020harder}. Early-5 averages predictions from five
early checkpoints on the original image. Static-5 averages five
deterministic views from one early checkpoint chosen on noisy validation
by accuracy, then NLL, then epoch. Both direct heads use the same frozen
features, architecture and fitting budget. A selected-single head uses the
canonical view from the checkpoint chosen for Static-5. Both five-pass
teachers are evaluated directly as secondary comparisons. The primary
contrast was Early-5 direct versus Static-5 direct, with a prespecified
threshold of 0.50 percentage points and a positive lower bound of the
paired 95\% interval.

The primary accuracy differences were $-1.00$, $+0.75$ and $+0.20$
points (mean $-0.02$, between-base SD $0.89$, conditional paired
95\% interval $[-0.53, 0.48]$). The primary criterion was not met.
Early-5 direct also averaged $-0.07$ points relative to the base
(nominal interval $[-0.63, 0.50]$). In the secondary comparison of the
uncompressed teachers, Early-5 averaged $+2.10$ points over Static-5
(nominal interval $[1.33, 2.87]$). The advantage observed for the
five-checkpoint teacher did not carry over to these direct heads.
The intervals condition on the three fitted bases and use the same
stratified resampled image IDs across predictors and seeds.
