\section{Conclusion}
Room for Doubt transfers an early training-margin summary to inference through class-conditioned regression on frozen base features. Matched scalar controls show higher accuracy for Early-5 than for the selected checkpoint margin or the mean of all 50 epochs in the evaluated runs. The gain appears on three independently trained CIFAR-100N bases and on two CIFAR-10N Worse bases, alongside improved ranking of original-view base errors. The two-base augmentation control retains a point-estimate advantage for Early-5, with clearer conditional accuracy intervals on CIFAR-10N. A T4 microbenchmark shows close timing to equal-view uniform TTA in this implementation, while the additional views remain costly. The evidence favors a compact early-history target under this recipe; calibration, causal interpretation and comparison with multi-model inference require separate assessment.
