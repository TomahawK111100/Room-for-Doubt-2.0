\section{Conclusion}
Room for Doubt transfers an early training-margin summary to inference through class-conditioned regression on frozen base features. Matched scalar controls show higher accuracy for Early-5 than for the selected checkpoint margin or the mean of all 50 epochs in the evaluated runs. The gain appears on two independently trained CIFAR-100N bases and on one CIFAR-10N Worse base, alongside improved ranking of original-view base errors. The evidence favors a compact early-history target under this training recipe, while calibration and the comparison with multi-model inference require separate assessment.
