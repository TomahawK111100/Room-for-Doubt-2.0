\section{Conclusion}
Room for Doubt transfers an early training-margin summary to inference through class-conditioned regression on frozen base features. Matched scalar controls show higher accuracy for Early-5 than for the selected checkpoint margin or the mean of all 50 epochs in the evaluated runs. The gain appears on three independently trained CIFAR-100N bases and on three CIFAR-10N Worse bases, alongside improved ranking of original-view base errors. The augmentation control retains an Early-5 advantage on all three CIFAR-100N bases and all three CIFAR-10N bases, with positive conditional accuracy intervals for seven of nine CIFAR-100N heads and all nine CIFAR-10N heads. Preliminary T4 timing is close to equal-view uniform TTA, while multiple views remain costly. Calibration, causal interpretation and comparison with multi-model inference require further study.
